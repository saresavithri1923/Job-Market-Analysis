# Job-Market-Analysis
An exploratory data analysis project focused on job market trends, in-demand skills, salary insights, and employment opportunities.

## Project Overview
The Job Market Analysis project focuses on understanding current employment trends, identifying in-demand job roles, analyzing salary patterns, and exploring the skills required by employers.

The objective is to gain insights into the job market and help job seekers understand career opportunities and industry requirements.

## Objectives
- Analyze job market trends.
- Identify the most demanded job roles.
- Explore salary distributions.
- Identify frequently requested skills.
- Understand employment opportunities across different industries.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Data Visualization

## Key Analysis
- Job role distribution
- Salary analysis
- Most demanded skills
- Industry-wise job opportunities
- Employment trends

## Project Outcomes
This project provides insights into job market requirements, popular career opportunities, salary trends, and the skills that employers are looking for.
